Methodology and algorithm mapping

The planning stack combines weighted skill importance, prerequisite feasibility, and mixed optimization to recommend the highest-value next steps.

Scoring logic

  • • Skill value is weighted by importance, career alignment, and learner level.
  • • Courses are filtered to respect prerequisite dependencies.
  • • The plan favors the highest-value sequence that closes the most critical gaps first.

Optimization strategy

  • • Mixed selection uses a greedy but constrained prioritization approach.
  • • Career paths map roles to required skills and milestone progressions.
  • • Progress prediction uses a lightweight k-nearest-neighbor style estimate over historical examples.
